- 2026-10-21 — Fixed the flaky `App.unreachable.test.tsx` Power-page test. Root cause: a page keeps refetching errored queries as panels mount (status fetched 3 times in the first 450 ms), so the test's "settled" check could land in a gap between two of them; the next fetch then started before the poll's `before` count and was still in flight when the fake interval fired, and TanStack deduplicated the interval's refetch into it, so no new status request was sent (`expected 3 to be greater than 3`). Test-only fix: `poll()` waits for `isFetching() === 0` and captures the count and advances in the same tick. No product change; the polls do not stop.
