- 2026-10-25 — ADR-0055 PR 6b: the planner groups machines into logical factories. A new pure module, `telemetry/services/factoryReduction.ts`, runs last in `productionGraph.ts` over the capped nodes, belt and pipe edges and transports (so the 2,000-transport cap still bounds it) and adds, per node, `factoryKey`, `shares` (only when the largest share is under 0.95: top four plus an `other` entry), `collapseUnder` (immediate post-dominator within the factory, Cooper-Harvey-Kennedy) and `role` (buffer, storage, depot, sink, power, export), and per response `factories` (name, products, side products, node keys, site keys, machine and shared-node counts) and `topologyHash`. Seeds are machines whose product leaves the supply graph (an `output` arc, directly or through buffers) or that have no supply out-arc, plus farms (extractors feeding no machine); a machine that reaches no seed is promoted to one. Seeds of one product whose upstream closures share a non-raw node are one factory. Shares are design-rate (max rates only): a destination's max demand split between its suppliers by max output, unknown arcs taking the remainder equally, excess and output arcs left out; a node's home is its largest share (ties: larger seed output, then lower key). A factory under 4 machines folds into another only as a dependent offshoot (Decision 6, option C): every supply input from that one factory, no extractor of its own, no output by transport, Depot or sink. Distance never enters. `ProductionGraphStats.factoryReduction` is logged by the route per request (factory count, factories per weak component, shared nodes, widest split, largest factory, unassigned groups, run time). `stronglyConnected` moved from `productionGraph.ts` to `connectionGraph.ts`. No contract change (PR 1 carried the fields); the frontend ignores them until PR 7 and 8.
