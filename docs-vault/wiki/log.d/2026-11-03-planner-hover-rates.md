- 2026-11-03 — Planner rates on hover and the highlight (ADR-0063 amendment, owner 2026-11-03). Not flagged; the demo shows it.
  - **Short rate.** The rate under a line's bubbles is `shortRateText` (`plannerText.ts`): "20/min", "~20/min" estimated, "≥20/min" at least, "?/min" unknown (drawn now; before, none), a fluid "20 m³/min". "Mixed" and "excess" are no longer drawn there. Every text path (titles, buttons, the outline, the legend's words) keeps the full words.
  - **When it shows.** With a mouse it is hidden at rest and rises in while its line is highlighted; a touch screen shows every one at rest. Its row is always reserved, so nothing moves.
  - **Highlight.** Pointing at a line (its label or a transparent wider stroke, `[data-line-hit]`) lights it and its two cards; pointing at a card lights all its lines and their far cards; the keyboard on a card's button or a line's bubble does the same. The rest dims to the item focus's quarter. Under item focus or the item filter it only shows the rates.
  - **Off the canvas.** `HighlightLayer.tsx` draws the dim (a canvas-coloured cover at the new `--opacity-graph-cover`, holed for what is lit) and the rates in its own composited `<svg>` over the canvas and the march layer. The pointer's target is in `pointerStore` (`lineHighlight.ts`), so pointing never re-renders, re-lays out or repaints the canvas. Measured at 4x CPU throttle on `planner-large`: p95 33.3 ms against 50 for the first cut inside the canvas.
  - **Layout.** Narrower label boxes (short text); an unknown line's box gains its rate row; the layout's height now holds a route that runs below dagre's box (a self-loop under a lone card).
  - **Legend.** A row with the four forms, read in words by a screen reader.
  - **Tests.** Unit tests for the format, the highlight and the store; RTL for the layer (the canvas's markup is unchanged by a hover); e2e for hovering a line and a card, reduced motion, touch at rest, and the hover frame times. Baselines of every state that draws the canvas change.
