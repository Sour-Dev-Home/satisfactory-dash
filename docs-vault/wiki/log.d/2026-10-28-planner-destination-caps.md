- 2026-10-28 — Planner destination caps (ADR-0062 Decision 1, build PR 3; frontend only, no contract change, no new dependency). A card's footer row now shows one cap per kind of end its flows reach that no line is drawn to: an overview factory card's destinations (storage, the Depot, the Sink, power, a station, unknown, or a newer backend's word) and, in an opened factory with Storage detail off, a group's or closed bubble's folded storage. A cap is a 14-unit stub into a 20-unit disc with the kind's building icon or glyph, a number badge past one flow, and past four kinds the first three and a "+k" cap. Its speed is `rateStep` on the cap's summed rate per unit (`sumCapRates`, ADR-0053 Decision 3's rule), the lines' function, so a line and its cap agree: measured caps pulse over a solid stub, estimated caps march their dashed stub, unknown caps are still and dotted, stalled caps are still (with the state's ring in marks mode). The moving part is drawn in the march layer, never the canvas (the #808 repaint), in its own group and mask, under its own budget `CAP_MARCH_MAX` (40), so caps never count against the lines' `MARCH_MAX`; Motion Off, reduced motion, a hidden tab, a glide or a lasso stop them, and the Motion control also shows when only caps can move. Text path: each cap's `<title>` (`capText`, "about 120 items/min to storage, 3 flows") and a roving-tabindex button per cap ("<card> sends ...") that shows the card's detail; `destinationChipText` stays in descriptions, the group panel and `FactoryFacts`. `cardSize` grows a capped card by one 28-unit row, so dagre and the router are unchanged; the new A/B benchmark (`layoutScene.caps.test.ts`, 48 cards) and the existing ones measured no layout cost (numbers in the ADR). New files `capModel.ts`, `capLayout.ts`, `DestinationCaps.tsx`, `rovingStop.ts` (the bubbles' `nextStop`, shared). Tests: `capModel.test.ts`, `capLayout.test.ts`, `SiteGraph.caps.test.tsx`, `layoutScene.caps.test.ts`; e2e: a caps frame-time test on the new `planner-caps-large` scenario (48 factories) with the lines' regression bound, and a keyboard case with axe, in `e2e/planner-graph.spec.ts`. Planner baselines with capped cards change (one row taller).
