- 2026-10-24 — The command bar's search chunk (cmdk) is preloaded once the app is idle (`lib/idle.ts`: a 2 s start delay, then requestIdleCallback with a 5 s timeout, or the delay alone on Safari), so the first Ctrl+K shows the search at once instead of "Loading search…". Nothing is preloaded under Save-Data or on 2G. Hovering or focusing the Search button also starts the chunk and the search's reads (servers, power, factory; `paletteQueries` and the shared `prefetchFresh` in `shell/pageQueries.ts`, which the tab prefetch now uses too). The preload, the hover and the bar share one import, and a failed one is cleared, so opening retries. The search was already local over cached data; this removes the one network wait it had. `e2e/palette-preload.spec.ts` checks the preload with no input, its absence under Save-Data, and no long task over 200 ms around it.
