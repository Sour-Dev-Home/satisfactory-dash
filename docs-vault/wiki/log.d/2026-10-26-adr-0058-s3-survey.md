- 2026-10-26 — ADR-0058 Sprint S3 header survey (`raw-sources/mod-native-readers-s3-headers-2026-10-26.md`). It covers R5 (rails) and R6 (conveyors: belts, lifts, pipes) and gives names and `file:line` only, written under the clean-room rules: FRM source not opened, no web search. It follows the S2 survey (dash PR #700).
  - **R5.** The railroad subsystem keeps its track list private, so tracks come from the buildable subsystem, filtered on `AFGBuildableRailroadTrack`. World-space points come from the track's spline component; FRM's roughly 75 cm sampling is not in the headers, so the native step is our choice. The captured world (65 segments) fits the layer's caps by a wide margin.
  - **R6.** Belts, lifts and pipelines come from the same buildable list. Each end is the connected component's owning building, so its `GetName()` equals the factory reader's machine id by construction. Pipes must be filtered on `AFGBuildablePipeline`, not the pipe base (hypertubes share it). Pipe capacity is the pipeline's flow limit in m3/s; belt and lift speed has no unit in the header and needs a read on the test server.
  - **Drone ports.** A belt end on a drone port names the port by the building's `GetName()`, the same form as FRM's drone-station id; which end delivers and which receives follows from the belt's own input and output ends. A capture of a belt on a port is still needed.
