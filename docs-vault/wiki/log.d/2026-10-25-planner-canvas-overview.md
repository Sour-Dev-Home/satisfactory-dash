- 2026-10-25 — ADR-0055 PR 8a (the first half of the canvas row, with ADR-0053 PR 6): the Planner's first level is now a factory canvas, and an opened factory is drawn as bubbles that open in place. `frontend/src/planner/graph/canvasScene.ts` (new, pure, no React or dagre) turns the graph and the viewer's choices into cards (a factory, a bubble of one recipe from one source tree, a group, the fold cap's "N more trees"), frames and summed flows (ADR-0053 Decision 3's weakest-quality rule, "at least" when a part has no number). The overview draws a card per factory (machines, where it stands, products, shared groups, "1 flow to storage" chips) and the cross-factory flows; choosing a card opens the factory. An opened factory starts open below `COLLAPSE_AT` (30) groups and collapsed above; a bubble opens in place into its groups inside a dashed frame with a Collapse button, "Collapse all" and "Expand all" flip every bubble, and a group still opens its machines panel.
