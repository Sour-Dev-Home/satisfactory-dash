- 2026-10-20 — Enrolment codes are limited to 20 an hour per account (ADR-0046 C7, task 5, #527), on top of the existing 10 a minute; both are checked before the transaction and refused with the usual 429 and `Retry-After` (the hourly message says "this hour", and an hourly refusal gives its minute slot back). The cap is `ENROLLMENT_CODES_PER_HOUR` (1 to 1000, default 20), validated at startup. The operator's account is exempt from the hourly cap only. As ADR-0046 specifies, it is a second in-memory `UserRateLimiter` (fixed window, per instance), so a restart resets it. Limits table in `runbooks/agents.md` updated.
