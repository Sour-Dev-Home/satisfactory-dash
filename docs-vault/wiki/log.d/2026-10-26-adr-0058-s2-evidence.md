  - **The readers.** R2a (factory machines), R2b (production rates) and R3 (trains and stations, with the `trainRoutes` layer) are read from the game itself. They passed `native-parity` once (run 40403583987, 30 minutes against FRM's endpoints, used as a black box) and `tick-cost-native` once (run 40396146628).

  - **Decisions along the way:**
