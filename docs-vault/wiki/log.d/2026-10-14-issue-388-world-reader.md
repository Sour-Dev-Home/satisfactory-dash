- 2026-10-14 — The edge agent gains a world reader (#388, ADR-0038 M4 part 1): `agent/src/worldReader.ts` reads rails every 10 min and resource nodes every 30 min (±10% jitter) through the game-adapter's `getRails`/`getResourceNodes`, and POSTs `{ observedAt, data }` to `/agent/v1/world/:layer` only when the SHA-256 of the projected data changed since the last accepted send (always once after start). A body over `MAP_WORLD_LAYER_MAX_BYTES` is skipped with one warning per streak; a 5xx or network error retries the same body with the snapshot push's backoff, a 4xx is not retried, a 401 stops the agent. `BackendClient.postWorldLayer` is the new call; `runAgent` takes an optional `world` (ports) and runs the reader beside its other loops. The CLI does not pass `world` yet, so the reader is not live on a player's PC until that wiring lands (follow-up issue).
