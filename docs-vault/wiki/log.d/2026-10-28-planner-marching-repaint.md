- 2026-10-28 — Planner marching dashes repaint only their own layer (ADR-0062 amendment build PR 3 follow-up; frontend only, no contract change, no new dependency). CI's frame-time run of #806 (headless Chromium, software raster, 210 lines) showed the cost did not follow the marching lines: desktop fitted view p95 116.7 ms with 4 lines marching against 16.8 with Motion Off, because a `stroke-dashoffset` animation inside the canvas `<svg>` repainted the whole canvas every frame. The marching strokes now draw in `MarchLayer` (`SiteGraph.tsx`), a second `<svg>` over the canvas with its viewBox, fit, border box and pan/zoom transform, `aria-hidden`, `pointer-events-none` and `will-change-transform` (its own compositing layer), rendered only while something marches. The canvas draws every line still, always; the layer draws an estimated line's moving dashes over a `stroke-surface` cover that hides its still dashes (stopping at the arrowhead's base), or a measured line's pulses, under a luminance mask with a hole for every label pill, card, bubble pile and the item card (`marchHoles` in `marching.ts`, with `CARD_STACK_OFFSET`). Marching also stops in script under reduced motion (Motion On too) and during a make-room glide. The e2e frame-time test now finds the marching lines in the layer, checks it lies on the canvas, and asserts on desktop that the fitted view's p95 is at most twice Motion Off's plus 17 ms. The ADR's "Values chosen in build PR 3" records the before-fix CI numbers and the fix; `frontend-planner-map.md` describes the layer.
