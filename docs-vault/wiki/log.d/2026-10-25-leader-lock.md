- 2026-10-25 — ADR-0054 PR 2: an advisory-lock leader for the background workers. New `platform/db/leaderLock.ts`: a dedicated `pg` client (not the pool) tries `pg_try_advisory_lock` every 5 s; the leader runs `SELECT 1` on it each tick, and any failure of that client (query error, 'error' event, dropped connection) stops the leader-only workers at once, then retries. Shutdown stops the leader workers, unlocks and closes the client; a dying process loses the lock with its session. Leader only: session purge, history maintenance, the agent-command and pending-server sweepers, the alert evaluator, alert delivery, and the pollers of servers the backend polls itself (`ServerRuntime.setLeader`; their one-shot workers are rebuilt on stepping down). Every instance: metrics flush, agent servers' history recorder and `LatestSnapshotWriter`, the event-loop monitor. The evaluator is built fresh each term and measures agent silence from its own start, so a new leader keeps the restart grace (D3.4). Without a database the process is always leader. The ADR-0052 upsert already had the recency guard (D3.5). Failover measured against Postgres: within one interval after a stop or a dropped connection. The Terraform overlap (PR 3) is applied only after this is live in production.
