- 2026-10-24 — ADR-0055 proposed (amends ADR-0053 and ADR-0050 Decisions 6 and 8): the Planner collapses the graph into logical factories named by final product, by a deterministic reduction (buffer folding, SCC condensation, seeds, design-rate shares, post-dominators) per weak component of the supply graph, so a remote farm joined by a train belongs to the factory it feeds, not the nearest site. Train, drone and truck routes are inferred from configuration read on the 10-minute world tier (trains first; storage contents, drones and trucks behind captures), never from per-belt rates; every flow names its destination, including the Dimensional Depot and the AWESOME Sink. Player-designated factories are stored per server, anchored by machine class and position. Storage detail and transport animation are per-viewer toggles.
