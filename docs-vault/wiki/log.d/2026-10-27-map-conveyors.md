The layer has no geometry: an edge carries end 0's position and the ids at its ends. New `conveyorsLayer.tsx` draws pipes, belts and lifts as three non-interactive multi-polylines over the power lines and under the buildings, in new `--color-map-pipe`/`--color-map-belt` tokens that point at the planner's pipe (`--color-info`) and belt (`--color-muted`) colours. Text "N belts, M pipes, K lifts on the map, L m of belt and P m of pipe measured, drawn straight between their ends." (only placed conveyors have a length; a kind with none placed says "pipe length unknown"; plus "N belts and pipes with an end the map can't place are drawn as a dot"; "shown" when cut), legend rows (Belt, Pipe, "Lift (vertical)", and "Belt or pipe, far end not placed" as a belt dot and a pipe dot side by side), contrast ratios in the belt and pipe token comments, and a "Belts, lifts and pipes in view" table (Kind, Tier, Length, From, To in words; 500 rows max; keyed id + index).
