- 2026-11-07 — The demo's guided tour no longer hides the Factory's sticky filter bar under its pinned tab row (ADR-0065 amendment), demo build only in effect.
  - Cause: while the tour runs, `.tour-pin` (the shell's tab row) sticks at `top: 0`, and the Factory's filter bar (ADR-0062 F.4; on a phone its search row) stuck at `top: 0` too, so on scroll it slid under the row.
  - Fix: the bar sticks at `top-sticky-top`, a new token `--spacing-sticky-top` (0 px, so nothing changes outside the tour or in production). `index.css`' `:root[data-guided-tour]` sets it to `--tour-pin-height`, which `TourHost.tsx` measures from the row with a ResizeObserver (the row wraps on phones) and writes to a constructed stylesheet in `document.adoptedStyleSheets`, removed on exit; no `style` attribute (CSP `style-src 'self'`). No other page has a top-sticky bar: the readings tables' headers stick inside their own scroll boxes, and the planner's undo toast sticks to the bottom.
  - Tests: `TourHost.test.tsx` (the row's height published while the tour runs, no `style` on `<html>`, the sheet gone on exit); `e2e/demo/tour.spec.ts` (on the Factory step, scrolled, the bar's top sits on the pinned row's bottom, desktop and phone).
