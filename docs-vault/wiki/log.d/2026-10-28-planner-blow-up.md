- 2026-10-28 — Planner blow-up in place (ADR-0061 build PR 4; frontend only, no contract change, no new dependency), behind `planner.nesting`. On the overview canvas a factory card blows up into a frame of its parts: a parent into its child factories (its own-machines card among them), an ordinary factory into its groups. Frames are dagre compound nodes, nested at most two deep (`roomForNestedTabs` keeps both header tabs clear); flows re-aggregate at the finest card drawn. The ADR's cap of 120 cards was measured and brought down to `EXPAND_MAX_CARDS = 60` (two deep: 61 cards 95 ms, 115 cards about 180 ms; Expand all at the cap 75 ms; the table is in the ADR's note); past it the Expand pill is disabled and says "Too large to expand here; open it". Mouse (Expand pill, frame Collapse), keyboard (hidden Expand and Collapse buttons, focus into the frame, Escape back to the card), toolbar Expand all / Collapse all, a glide by the existing tween (off under reduced motion), and the canvas's name lists what is blown up. Remembered per path for the session (`foldMemory` `expanded`). Opening a card drawn inside a frame pushes its chain onto `?open=` (`pathBelow`). With the flag off nothing changes. New tests: scene, layout (nesting, memo, benchmark), RTL, foldMemory, pathBelow, and an e2e keyboard case with axe in `planner-graph.spec.ts`.
