- 2026-10-28 — Planner blow-up in place (ADR-0061 build PR 4; frontend only, no contract change, no new dependency), behind `planner.nesting`. On the overview canvas a factory card blows up into a frame of its parts: a parent into its child factories (its own-machines card among them), an ordinary factory into its groups. Frames are dagre compound nodes, nested at most two deep (`roomForNestedTabs` puts a nested frame's top `NESTED_FRAME_RISE` = 44 px below its parent's, so the header tabs keep apart and the two Collapse pills' touch targets never meet); flows re-aggregate at the finest card drawn. The ADR's cap of 120 cards was measured and brought down to `EXPAND_MAX_CARDS = 60` (two deep: 61 cards 95 ms, 115 cards about 180 ms; Expand all at the cap 75 ms; the table is in the ADR's note); past it the Expand pill is drawn disabled and says "Too large to expand here; open it", and it and its hidden button open the factory; the toolbar's Expand all, disabled when nothing fits, says why. An Expand pill clicked while the keyboard is on the canvas moves focus into the frame. Mouse (Expand pill, frame Collapse), keyboard (hidden Expand and Collapse buttons, focus into the frame, Escape back to the card), toolbar Expand all / Collapse all, a glide by the existing tween (off under reduced motion), and the canvas's name lists what is blown up. Remembered per path for the session (`foldMemory` `expanded`). Opening a card drawn inside a frame pushes its chain onto `?open=` (`pathBelow`). With the flag off nothing changes. New tests: scene, layout (nesting, memo, benchmark), RTL, foldMemory, pathBelow, and an e2e keyboard case with axe in `planner-graph.spec.ts`.
