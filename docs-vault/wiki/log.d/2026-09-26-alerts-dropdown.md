- 2026-09-26 — Frontend, ADR-0027 PR 9 (the owner's redesign): the header bell now opens a dropdown instead of a full Alerts page. It shows the delivery and mute notes, what's firing first, and then the recent log, and each alert expands in place for its detail (a disclosure button). "Show older alerts" pages by `nextBefore`, and "Manage alerts" links to the Alerts section on Settings. It's a non-modal disclosure like the account menu (ADR-0016 item 8): Escape closes it and returns focus to the bell, as does a click outside or tabbing out. It fits the viewport at 390 px and scrolls. The alert log is read only while the dropdown is open (`queries.alertEvents` has a one-minute staleTime and no background poll); the badge's status poll is unchanged. `/app/alerts` now redirects to `/app/settings#alerts`, and the old page and its panels are removed. The dropdown's size and stacking come from new tokens in `index.css` (`--z-popover`, `--popover-width`, `--popover-max-height`).
