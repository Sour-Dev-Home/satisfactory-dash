- 2026-09-26 — Frontend, ADR-0031 PR 4: a change to auto-pause on a server reached through the game PC's agent. The PUT answers 202 with a command, and the Settings page now follows it. It shows "Saving…" and holds the toggle while the command is on its way, polling `GET /commands/:commandId` about once a second (`queries.command`, stopping at a final status). On success it re-reads the setting. On failure it says why, by the agent's result code, with a general line for an unknown code. On expiry, or with no final answer by just after `expiresAt` (a status this build doesn't know counts as still on its way), it says "The game PC didn't answer in time; the setting didn't change." and re-reads. A local server's 200 behaves as before. New scenarios `settings-relayed-saving` and `settings-relayed-failed`, and e2e cases.
