- 2026-10-27 — Planner: "Merge into one" and Split no longer refuse nearly every factory as "too large" (the owner, 2026-10-27). A group's `count` also counts conveyor ends no Factory-read building matches (storage, stations, sinks, generators, stale ends), which list no machine id and are never designation members (the backend designates machine buildings only), so `machineIds.length < count` was almost always true; a group is now "listed in part" only when its ids reached `PLANNER_MAX_MACHINE_IDS`, the cap the backend cuts at (`listedInPart` in `designationMatch.ts`). Extractor cards (water and every miner: getFactory lists no extractor, ADR-0050 "Ends only", and `getExtractor` is not read) now show the rate their links carry, summed, in m³/min on pipes and items/min on belts ("Water · 180 m³/min"; "about" in the tooltip, Tables and side panel when any link is an estimate), or "rate not read" when a link has no number. The backend no longer gives an extractor's link a guessed 0 when it shares its destination with a machine: with no output data its share is unknown. No contract change.
