- 2026-10-28 — Frontend (ADR-0061 build PR 3, planner factory hierarchy): the factory tree, its levels and the open path.
  - **Flag:** behind `planner.nesting` in `plannerPrefs.ts` (Decision 7), off by default with no control; off, every factory is top level as before. Build PR 6 removes it after the owner's review on the demo.
  - **Tree:** `planner/factoryTree.ts` (pure) nests the graph's flat factories by the designations' `parentId`, or the graph's `parentDesignationId` while the list has not answered. A parent with no machines of its own has no graph factory, so it is keyed `factory|designation|<id>` and named from the list (Decision 2). A missing parent makes its child top level, a cycle is cut at its smallest id, anything past `PLANNER_DESIGNATION_MAX_DEPTH` hangs under its level-2 ancestor, and a designation with no machines below it is left out.
  - **Roll-ups:** a parent's card is a `FactoryModel` over its parts: machine and state counts summed, products its own and its children's minus the flows between them, clamped at 0 (a product used up inside is dropped), and "at least" (`FactoryModel.atLeast`, `outputLine`, `productSummary`) where such a flow is estimated or unknown. A parent's own machines are one more part, "<name> (own machines)", keyed `<key>|own`, so every machine is drawn once.
  - **Scenes:** `overviewScene(graph, models, path, tree)` shows the parts of the path's last parent (the top level for an empty path), each group owned by its shown card (`levelOwner`); `Scene.path`, and the canvas is named "Factories in <parent>". `SiteGraph.tsx` only passes `path` and `tree` through its overview target. `factoryModelOf` is split out of `factoryModels` in `hierarchy.ts`.
  - **Path:** `?open=a&open=b`, root first, read with `getAll`, so a #776 link with one key is a path of one. The longest valid prefix is kept; a bad tail is replaced in the address once the designation list has answered, with "That factory is no longer here." in a `role="status"` line. Opening pushes one level; Back ("All factories", "Back to <parent>") steps back through history when the Planner pushed the entry, else replaces the address with one level up. A breadcrumb (`nav` "Factory path", buttons, `aria-current="page"` on the last) shows once anything nests, and a level change focuses its heading.
  - **Panel:** the Tables view lists the shown level's cards and the flows between them; a parent or own-machines card shows only the designated marker (editing is PR 5), and merge picks only plain factories at the top level.
  - **Tests:** `factoryTree.test.ts` (roll-ups with and without own machines, 3 levels, orphan, cycle, depth, paths, scenes) and `PlannerView.hierarchy.test.tsx` (a #776 link, drilling down, Back, breadcrumb, a trimmed tail, focus, the flag off), over the small world in `src/test/plannerTree.ts`.
