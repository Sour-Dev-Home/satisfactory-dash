Your servers

Only connect servers you have the right to administer. You're responsible for the access tokens and setup codes you create, and for who you invite: the people you invite see your server's data.

You are responsible for telling the people who play on your server, and for having the right to share this data. The setup screen has a short message you can copy for them. See the Privacy Policy.

Limits

Currently, each account may add up to 2 servers. Only 1 of them may be waiting to connect at a time, and a server that isn't connected within 24 hours is removed. You can add up to 5 servers a day and ask for up to 20 setup codes an hour. The service as a whole has room for a limited number of servers, and new sign-ups are limited per hour, so a request can be refused when it is full. The operator can change these limits at any time. Lowering a limit never removes a connected server you already have.
