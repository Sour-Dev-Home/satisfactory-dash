Satis Manager

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Satis Manager is a free dashboard for a Satisfactory dedicated server. This page says what it collects, why, who else handles it, how long it's kept, and what you can ask for. It describes what the service actually does. It offers the same rights to everyone, wherever you live, and it doesn't claim compliance with any particular law.

Who runs this

Satis Manager is run by an individual, Leonardo Nunez, who decides how its data is used. Contact: privacy@satis-manager.com.

What we collect, and why

Data Why
Your Google account id and verified email address Signing you in, and matching you to invitations
A display name (one you choose, or taken from your email) Shown to the people you share a server with
Session records (a hashed session id and its times) Keeping you signed in, and "sign out everywhere"
Server names, memberships and roles Sharing a server and deciding who may do what
Game data: power, production, machine states, building positions The dashboard. It describes the game world, not you.
Audit events: who did what to which server, and when (no emails or names in them) Security, and investigating abuse
Your IP address Rate limiting (kept in memory only) and security logs of sign-in events

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No selling of your data, and no sharing of it for marketing.
  • No third party tracks you on this site, and we don't track you across other sites. So a browser's "Do Not Track" signal changes nothing here: there's no tracking for it to turn off.
  • Cookies: only the ones the service needs to work. The session cookie keeps you signed in, and a short-lived cookie (10 minutes) protects the Google sign-in. No cookie banner, because there's nothing optional to accept.

Who else handles data

  • Google: signing in. You share your email address and account id with us through it.
  • Cloudflare: hosting, DNS, the network tunnel and the firewall. It sees every request's IP address and metadata.
  • Amazon Web Services: stores our encrypted backups.

When a new feature needs another provider (for example, alert delivery), it's added here before that feature goes live.

These providers are based in the United States, so your data is processed there.

How long it's kept

  • Your account: until you delete it.
  • Sessions: session records are deleted 30 days after the session expires (a session lasts at most 8 hours).
  • Game data: not stored. It's read from your server when the dashboard asks; the only history is the last 5 minutes of power readings, kept in memory for the chart.
  • Backups: Amazon Web Services stores encrypted backups for 30 days, so deleted data can survive in backups for up to 30 days.
  • Audit events: 1 year. They're kept for security and hold no emails or names.
  • Server logs, including IP addresses on sign-in events: 14 days.

Your choices and rights

Whoever and wherever you are, you can ask to see the data we hold about you, get a copy of it, correct it, or delete it, and you can object to how it's used. Email privacy@satis-manager.com from the address on your account, and we'll answer within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes your user record, your sign-in identity, your sessions and your memberships. A server you own is handed over or deleted first. You can also stop using Google sign-in for us at any time from your Google account settings.

Security

User accounts sign in with Google, so we don't store passwords for them. Session ids are stored hashed; the database gives each part of the service only the access it needs; the site runs under a strict Content Security Policy; and the API can only be reached through Cloudflare. No system is perfectly secure, but these are the efforts we make. If a breach affects your data, we'll tell you without undue delay.

Children

Satis Manager isn't directed at children, and you must be at least 13 to use it. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 13, we delete it.

Changes to this policy

The date at the top changes with every update. For a significant change, we'll also put a notice on the site before it takes effect.
