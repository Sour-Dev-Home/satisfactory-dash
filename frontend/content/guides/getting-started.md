Satis Manager is a dashboard for a Satisfactory dedicated server. It reads the server while you play and shows the
factory's state in the browser: power production and consumption for each circuit, every machine's recipe and
output rate, and which machines are backed up or paused. You can check on the factory from a phone or a second
screen without opening the game.

## Try the live demo

The quickest way to look around is the [live demo](https://demo.satis-manager.com). It runs the real dashboard on a
made-up factory, needs no account and sends nothing to a game server, so you can open every page and see what each
one shows before connecting your own.

## Sign in

Sign-in is invite-only during the beta. Once you have an account, open [the app](/app) and sign in. The dashboard
opens on the Overview, which sums up the server, power and factory in one place; the tabs along the top open each
area in detail.
