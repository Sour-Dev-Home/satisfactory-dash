# Contributing

Outside contributions aren't being accepted yet.

This project is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only (see
`LICENSE`), and the copyright holder wants to keep the option of also offering commercial
terms (ADR-0018). That needs every outside contributor to grant relicensing rights first,
through a contributor license agreement (CLA), or a DCO sign-off plus a written relicensing
grant. Neither is set up yet, so pull requests from outside contributors will be closed
without review until one is.

Issues (bug reports and ideas) are welcome.
